The name in the window

Our mark is a stained-glass window. Choose a part to see it up close.

Chi and Rho, written X and P, are the first two letters of “Christ” in Greek. Christians have set them together as one sign since the fourth century, and the company is named after it.

Alpha is the first letter of the Greek alphabet. In Revelation, Christ calls himself the Alpha and the Omega: the first and the last.

Omega is the last letter of the Greek alphabet. Set either side of the Chi Rho, the two letters say that Christ is at the beginning and the end of everything, our work included.

The lead that holds the glass is drawn as circuit traces, each ending in a solder pad: an old window made from the stuff our software runs on.
