Machines and moving a licence

  • Adding your key to a machine uses one of its machine slots. The program checks with our payment provider, Polar, when it starts and once a day.
  • You can move a licence: “Release this machine” in the program frees its slot. A machine must hold its slot for 7 days before it can release it.
  • If a machine is lost, wiped or reinstalled and still holds a slot, email help@chirhocode.com and we free it for you.

Offline use

  • The program keeps working offline. A successful check is good for 30 days; the program warns you during the last 7. After 30 days without a check, the machine runs as Free until it can check again.
  • If your computer’s clock is wrong (earlier than the last check), nothing changes until the next successful check.

When a machine falls back to Free

A machine falls back to Free when its licence is no longer valid. For example: you released it, the key was refunded or revoked, or it was offline for more than 30 days.

  • If the key stops being valid while the machine is online, you get 24 hours’ warning first.
  • On Free, a machine runs at most 2 game servers. You can pick which 2 keep running; otherwise the 2 oldest do. The others are put on a licence hold: stopped cleanly with the world saved, and kept stopped.
  • A held server is never deleted. You can still view it, read its log, back it up, ban players on it and remove it. You can’t start, restart, restore or reconfigure it until the hold lifts.
  • The hold lifts when the machine is licensed again or runs 2 servers or fewer. Held servers then stay stopped until you start them.
  • A machine that had 3 or more servers before 1.0 gets the same 24 hours’ warning.

What the program sends

To check a licence, the program sends Polar your licence key, a label (the machine’s host name) and a one-way hash of the machine’s ID, plus the program’s version. It sends nothing about your game servers, players or worlds. There are no analytics or trackers.
