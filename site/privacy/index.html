This website

  • No analytics, no cookies and no trackers. The pages have no scripts, and every font, image and stylesheet is served from this site, so your browser asks no one else for anything.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To serve the pages, GitHub receives the usual details of each request, such as your IP address, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don’t get those logs.

Server Sanctuary

  • When it checks a licence (from version 1.0: when it starts and once a day), the app sends Polar only the licence key, the machine’s host name as a label, a one-way hash of the machine’s ID, and the app’s version. It sends nothing about your servers, players or worlds.
  • The app has no analytics or telemetry. What it keeps about your servers and players (ban lists, the Players screen) stays on your machine.
  • Other network use, none of it to us: to show players your public address, the app sends one STUN request to Cloudflare (or Google, if Cloudflare doesn’t answer) and keeps the answer in memory only; Podman downloads each game’s container image; and the game servers download their games from Steam.

Payments

  • Polar (polar.sh) is the merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles tax and sends the receipt, under Polar’s privacy policy. Your card details go to Polar, never to us.
  • We can see your order and the email you bought with, so we can help with your licence.

Email

If you write to help@chirhocode.com, we use your address and message only to answer you. Our mail is hosted by Proton Mail.
